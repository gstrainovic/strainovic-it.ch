# Donate

5-centime rounding and UID Check Switzerland are free. If a plugin saves you work and
you would like to give something back, I appreciate a donation. You choose the amount.

![QR code of a Swiss QR-bill for a donation to Goran Strainovic, amount of your choice](/img/spenden-qr.svg)

## How it works

- Scan the QR code with your banking app, enter the amount and confirm
- Or transfer by hand: CH06 0878 1000 2618 1360 0, Swissquote Bank SA, Gland
  (BIC SWQBCHZZXXX), in favour of Goran Strainovic, Bahnstrasse 9b, 9323 Steinach,
  reference “Spende Plugin”

The donation is voluntary and not a condition for support. I answer questions about
the plugins either way.
