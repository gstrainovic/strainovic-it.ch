# Website enquiries straight into bexio

**No more retyping.** Every enquiry from your contact or quote form lands in bexio as
a contact, with a quote straight away if you like. You reply from within bexio, with all
the data, no copy and paste.

![Settings page in the WordPress admin: connection to bexio established, log with a new contact and a note on an existing contact](/img/bexio-connector-einstellungen-en.png)

## What it does

- Works with Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms and Elementor Forms
- Creates company and contact person as a bexio contact, with address, email, phone
  and the form text as a note
- Recognises existing contacts by their email address, no duplicates
- Pro: creates a quote or an order from the form fields, assigns the contact to an
  employee and creates a follow-up task
- Log in the WordPress admin: what was transferred and when, with the error message
  if bexio could not be reached

## Who it is for

Trades businesses, consultancies, agencies and service providers in Switzerland who
use bexio and receive enquiries or quote requests on their website. Worth it from five
enquiries a week.
