# Zefix/UID check for WooCommerce and WordPress forms

**Business customers checked in seconds.** The customer types in their UID, the Swiss
company identification number. The plugin checks it with the federal register, fetches
company name and address from Zefix and fills in the form. Wrong or deleted companies
never get as far as buying on invoice.

![WooCommerce checkout with UID field: the UID entered is not in the UID register, the order is rejected](/img/uid-check-checkout-fehler-en.png)

## What it does

- UID field in the WooCommerce checkout and in contact, quote and registration forms
  (Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Elementor Forms)
- Check digit and format instantly, status "active" or "deleted" via the federal
  UID web service
- Company name, legal form, registered office and address from Zefix, filled in automatically
- VAT registration visible, UID stored in the order and the customer account
- Buying on invoice only for valid companies
- Change alert: customer deleted or moved, you are the first to know

## Who it is for

Swiss online shops with business customers, B2B shops with purchase on invoice,
agencies with many WooCommerce clients. Not a replacement for a credit check, but the
step before it, which today is done by hand or not at all.
